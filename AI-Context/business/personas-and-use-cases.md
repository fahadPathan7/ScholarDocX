# Personas And Use Cases

## Persona 1: Focused Researcher

Applies mainly to PhD or research-heavy Master's programs.

Needs:

- Track professors, labs, research interests, and publications.
- Connect target professors to specific universities and programs.
- Draft research proposals and professor-specific emails.
- Manage funding opportunities and assistantships.
- Compare country-specific requirements.

Primary use cases:

- Build a shortlist of professors by research fit.
- Draft tailored cold emails.
- Maintain proposal versions per institution.
- Track replies and follow-up reminders.

## Persona 2: Broad Applicant

Applies to many Bachelor's or Master's programs.

Needs:

- Track many universities in one place.
- Manage deadlines, test scores, transcripts, and application portals.
- Store reusable documents.
- See all pending work from one dashboard.

Primary use cases:

- Build a country and university pipeline.
- Track application status through a Kanban board.
- Link static documents to each application.
- View upcoming deadlines chronologically.

## Persona 3: Hybrid Applicant

Applies to Master's and PhD programs at the same time.

Needs:

- Separate application types without losing global visibility.
- Reuse documents across tracks.
- Track different workflows for coursework and research applications.
- Compare outcomes across degree levels and countries.

Primary use cases:

- Enable multiple degree workspaces.
- See combined deadline and progress dashboard.
- Maintain different SOP/proposal versions per application type.
- Track professor outreach for research programs only.
