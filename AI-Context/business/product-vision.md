# Product Vision

ScholarDocX is a locally hosted, privacy-first application management portal for higher education applicants.

It helps users coordinate complex international application cycles for Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD programs by consolidating:

- Application tracking
- University and program research
- Professor and lab research
- Deadline management
- Document drafting
- Static file storage
- Email outreach
- AI-assisted strategic support

## Core Promise

ScholarDocX reduces the cognitive load of higher education applications without forcing users to surrender private academic documents to a remote platform.

## Product Principles

- Secure personal workspace data ownership.
- Privacy over convenience.
- Structured application planning over scattered spreadsheets.
- Integrated document and outreach workflows.
- AI assistance as an optional accelerator, not the system of record.
- Zero required infrastructure cost.
- AI-DLC friendly architecture and documentation.

## Out Of Scope Unless Approved

- Hosted multi-user SaaS.
- Cloud database storage.
- Social networking or public applicant profiles.
- University-side admissions management.
- Paid hosted AI proxy services.
- Automatic email sending without explicit user configuration.
