# Business Context

This folder explains why ScholarDocX exists and which business decisions guide the product.

## Files

- [product-vision.md](product-vision.md): core product vision and goals.
- [personas-and-use-cases.md](personas-and-use-cases.md): target users and primary use cases.
- [business-requirements.md](business-requirements.md): numbered business requirements.
- [decisions.md](decisions.md): business decision log.
- [authentication-position.md](authentication-position.md): business stance on signup/signin and Google OAuth.
- [success-metrics.md](success-metrics.md): product success measures.
- [assumptions-and-risks.md](assumptions-and-risks.md): assumptions, risks, and open questions.

## When To Update

Update this folder when work changes:

- Target users
- Core value proposition
- Privacy expectations
- Cost expectations
- Product scope
- Success metrics
- Business decisions
